# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

DearBot contributors are expected to be respectful, constructive, and specific. Debate technical decisions vigorously without attacking people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberate disclosure of private career or applicant data are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository discussions, reviews, community spaces, and private project communication when representing the project.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Security vulnerabilities and accidental data exposure should instead follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed confidentially and handled proportionately.

Project maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

This policy is adapted from the principles of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
